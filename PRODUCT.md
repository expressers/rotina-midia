# Rotina Mídia

> Product context for design work. Inferred from Eládio's brief (18 Oct 2026) and the current site rotinamidia.com.br; assumptions are marked.

## What it is
Audiovisual production company working between Luanda (Angola) and São Paulo (Brazil). Director: Fernando Filó. Video and photography, from script to edit.

## Work
- Campaigns: EntraNaZona, Afrimoney × Bulusinha & Cleyton M, Africell, Conhecer Angola com o BAI.
- Corporate and trade fairs: Natal com BAI 2025, Prémio Sirius Deloitte 2024, FILDA 2025, US–Africa Business Summit 2025, Angotic 2025, FIB 2025, Etu Energias.
- Music videos: Dioguito "Tânia", Muloji, Black Spygo & TRX "Devolve", Yola Semedo "Gaguejar".
- Documentary: Back2Black (Rio de Janeiro).
- Weddings and shoots: Suleyma & Newton, Nadiuska & Ed, shoots in Bauru-SP.

## Audience (assumption)
Marketing and comms leads at Angolan banks, telcos, energy companies and event organisers; artists and labels; couples. They judge by the films. They decide by watching, then they call or WhatsApp.

## Job of the homepage
Prove craft in the first viewport, let visitors watch real films without leaving the page, and make contact (WhatsApp, phone, email) one step away.

## Brand
- Logo: AFs.zip from Alberto Charamba. Wordmark ROTINA plus a motion mark (two slices and a circle). Mark red #bf0811 grading to near-black.
- Colours: ground #0b0a09, text #f3efe8, accent brand red #bf0811 (confirmed by Eládio over the orange in the first brief).
- Type: Barlow Condensed 700 (display) + Barlow (text).
- Voice: direct, warm, cinematic. Portuguese (Angolan/European spelling, assumption; the old site mixes PT-BR).
