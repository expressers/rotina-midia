# Surface brief: homepage

Mode: Experience (the films lead), with a Persuade close (contact).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a reel being projected. Scroll is the playhead: a SMPTE timecode in the header counts frames as you scroll, and a strip of 16:9 frames advances through the hero. It refuses the category default of a muted autoplay video behind a centred slogan.

OWN-WORLD: Projection-room black #0b0a09, warm print white #f3efe8, the logo's red #bf0811 used only for the playhead, the play mark and live states. Barlow Condensed 700 set very large and tight like title cards; Barlow for credits. Film-strip perforations and edge print as the only ornament. Square corners everywhere.

STORY: See the work immediately, understand it's a Luanda/São Paulo house led by a director, watch a film in place, scan the filmography, read what clients say, then WhatsApp or call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: "Da rotina" pinned top-left and "ao cinema." pinned bottom-right at ~15vw. Across the middle, a full-width strip of 16:9 frames on perforated film; the centre frame carries a large red play mark that opens the showreel. One line of copy and contacts sit under the strip on the left.

FORM: Pinned poster (brief-pinned direction, taste-skill). No concept roll: the brief pins the direction.

SIGNATURE: Scroll-linked timecode + frame strip. Full-screen work panels pin and stack; title / category / location sit on the centre line; "Ver filme" opens a nocookie player.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
